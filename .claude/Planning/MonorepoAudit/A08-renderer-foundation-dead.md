# A08 — Renderer Foundation: Dead, Stale, and Obsolete

Scope: `src/renderer/PommoraUIX/**`, `Animation/`, `Assets/`, `Interactions/`, `Utilities/`, `Testing/`, and the renderer root files. All paths below are relative to `Pommora/src/renderer/` unless prefixed.

Method: every export in scope was word-counted across `src/` (tests excluded; Showcase tallied separately), and every zero or one was verified by reading the call sites. Component props were audited by grepping JSX attributes on every opening tag. CSS custom properties and classes were audited declared-vs-read. The one numeric claim (4.1) was reproduced in Node. Counts exclude comments.

Legend: **Conf** = confidence that the item is removable as described. **Breaks** = what happens if the finding is wrong or the removal is done naively.

## Summary

The foundation is mostly alive; the rot is at the edges and in duplicated definitions rather than whole dead files. Highest-value removals: the `paneMaterial` glass recipe is a byte-identical hand-unrolled copy of `frostStyle({...SURFACE_FROST, brightness: 95})` (proven); the curated icon registry's tree-shaking rationale is false because `allSymbols.ts` imports every Lucide icon anyway, three app sites already bypass it, and six of its entries have no reader at all; Tabler is namespace-imported with a computed key, shipping the whole library for 23 glyphs. The drag engine carries ~45 lines of configurability (`swap`, `bounds`, `modifiers`, `canReorder`, notify callbacks) that only the Showcase Lab exercises, and `SortableZone`'s `layout` prop is discarded yet passed at nine call sites. `PickerMenu`'s non-self-managed branch is unreachable (all 27 callers pass `open`). Twenty-one bridge CSS vars, the `solid` tint step, the `callout` icon size, and 26 of 40 emitted typography classes have zero readers. Three copies of "hold last value" and two of "toggle a set" exist. Motion has two spellings plus JS mirrors that drifted (`240` vs `280`; an "out" token that no longer exists). Showcase-only exports: `arraySwap`, `Row`, `errorRing`, `shapes`, four Labels re-exports. `nativeCaret.ts` (359 lines) is the heaviest host-specific item and a product decision.
